Bracer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Short version: Bracer stores all your personal data (photos, treatment info) exclusively on your device. We do not upload, sell, or share your photos or health data with anyone.

1. Who We Are

Bracer is an orthodontic treatment tracking app developed by Heptasol Technologies. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at heptasoltechnologies@gmail.com.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device using local storage. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • Progress photos you take or import
  • Treatment start date and treatment type (braces / aligners)
  • Pain level logs and milestone notes
  • Reminder and notification preferences
  • Aligner wear time records
  • App settings and preferences

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

3. AI Assistant

When you use the AI assistant feature, the messages you type are sent to Anthropic, Inc. to generate a response. Anthropic processes these messages according to their own privacy policy. We recommend not sharing sensitive personal or medical information in the chat.

Anthropic Privacy Policy: anthropic.com/privacy

4. Subscription & Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat may collect your App Store purchase history and an anonymous device identifier to verify your subscription. No payment card information is ever shared with us.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Push Notifications

If you enable reminders, your device's push notification token is used by Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) to deliver notifications locally on your device. This token is not stored on our servers.

6. Camera & Photo Library Access

Bracer requests access to your camera and photo library solely to let you capture and import progress photos. Photos are saved directly to your device's local storage and are never uploaded.

7. Analytics & Advertising

Bracer does not use any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not collect crash reports or usage statistics.

8. Children's Privacy

Bracer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding your data: heptasoltechnologies@gmail.com
